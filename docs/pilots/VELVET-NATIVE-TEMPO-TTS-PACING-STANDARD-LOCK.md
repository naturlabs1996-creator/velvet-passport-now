# Velvet Passport — Native Voice Pacing Standard (LOCK)
Approved by director: 2026-10-19

Applies to all production routes, French and English, including the 61-route pipeline.

- Chatterbox Multilingual V3: **native tempo 1.00**. Do not apply digital time stretching.
- Use deliberate punctuation in a **separate TTS performance layer**, only where appropriate: commas for light breaths, full stops for clear boundaries, paragraphing for dramatic structure.
- Explicit pauses should be managed independently, not forced by distortive speed changes.
- Punctuation behavior must be tested by listening; it is not a precise timing guarantee.
- Preserve every approved **editorial LOCK** script untouched. TTS-layer punctuation edits require director approval if they change narrative meaning, and must never silently overwrite editorial source.
- Keep approved voice references: FR male default, FR female candidate 02; EN male p232, EN female p239. Their artistic approvals do not substitute for commercial rights clearance.
- QA gates: verify listening, natural articulation, no metallic 'canisse' effect, measured stop durations, and any pause/timing changes. Do not substitute a tempo-adjusted older master for new native-tempo production.
