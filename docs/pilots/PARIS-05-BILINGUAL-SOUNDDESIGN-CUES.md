# Paris 05 — Sound-design cue register (FR + EN)

Status: PRODUCTION PLAN — effects not yet mixed or rights-cleared.

## Non-negotiable audio rules
- Make two separate mixes aligned to the final **FR and EN voice masters**, not a single timing copied across languages.
- Narration first: sound effects duck automatically under speech; no sound may obscure place names, directions or historic qualifications.
- Soundscape is a contemporary reconstruction of plausible historic ambience, **never represented as an authentic period recording**.
- Prefer documented, legally reusable or original recordings; store URL, creator, licence, commercial-use permission, attribution, source WAV and processing history for every sound.
- No loud surprise sounds near streets; avoid disorienting binaural cues for pedestrians. Keep an accessible narration-forward mix.
- Use discrete sounds tied to scripted moments, gentle fade-ins/outs, and meaningful moments of silence; never bed all scenes in constant noise.
- Restrict invented specifics: no fabricated dialogue, last words, gunfire pattern, or claims that a particular sound was recorded at the historic location.

## QA gates
- FR voice / EN voice complete and QA-accepted independently.
- Effect licences, provenance, and archive-versus-reconstruction labelling recorded.
- Speech intelligibility audition for both languages at typical phone-earbud volume and with environmental street noise.
- Headphone safety: avoid sharp loud transients; measure integrated loudness, true peak and ducking; preserve dynamic safety margin.
- Cue timing checked against final FR and final EN masters, not approximate script timings.
- Two final exports, each with its own audio mix and subtitles.
