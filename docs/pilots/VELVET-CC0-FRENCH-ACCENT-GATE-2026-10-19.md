## Mandatory source gate before any new Chatterbox FR audition
1. Identify and document a **specific** audio source WAV URL whose voice has already been independently auditioned or whose spoken pronunciation has been verified to match metropolitan French. Preserve evidence, not a vague inferred nationality.
2. Establish source rights: CC0/permission PLUS applicable donation consent or speaker agreement for synthesizing commercially; no voice-cloning on an unlicensed source.
3. Record candidate in an auditable manifest with source ID, exact downloadable URL, licence, documentary proof, language/accent listening check by a competent French listener, role male/female based on vocal timbre as heard, and approval-to-audition status.
4. Only entries with all gates PASS may be sent to Chatterbox V3. **Do not generate new random donor IDs**, and do not imply that unknown metadata establishes nationality/accent.
5. Chatterbox 1.00 native tempo, no time-stretch, and editorial LOCK source unchanged.
