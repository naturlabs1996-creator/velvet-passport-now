# NOW Hotel Partner Brief — Grand Hôtel du Palais Royal

Status: APPROVED by NOW Lodging Trust Gate
Commercial priority: Wave 1
Prepared: 2026-08-30

## Why this hotel

Grand Hôtel du Palais Royal is a strong first-wave NOW partner candidate because its guest-service model is unusually aligned with the product. The hotel positions itself as a 5-star boutique property in central Paris, immediately beside the Palais Royal, Louvre and Tuileries. Its official website currently states 59 rooms and suites. SLH currently lists 54 rooms; for this brief we use the property’s own current figure and keep the discrepancy flagged for future verification.

The hotel has an active Clefs d’Or concierge operation led by Christopher El Ouadakri and explicitly invites guests to use the concierge before arrival and during the stay. This makes NOW complementary rather than competitive: NOW can extend high-quality guest guidance when the guest is already outside the hotel, between planned moments, after concierge hours, or when plans change in real time.

## Partner proposition

Positioning line:

**Your hotel takes care of the guest while they’re here. NOW helps take care of them when they step outside.**

NOW is not another generic Paris guide. It helps the traveler decide what to do next using immediate context such as location, available time, weather, transit conditions, reservations, proximity and practical needs. Recommendations are separated from provider inventory and pass through NOW’s trust controls before being presented as NOW recommendations.

## Why it fits the Grand Hôtel du Palais Royal

1. Central location creates a high density of real-time guest decisions: Louvre, Palais Royal, Tuileries, Seine, theatre, restaurants, shopping and onward routes.
2. Strong concierge culture means the hotel already values curated guidance and personalized guest experience.
3. International luxury clientele is a natural fit for a multilingual, mobile-first city companion.
4. A boutique scale makes a controlled pilot practical without requiring a large operational rollout.
5. NOW can reduce repetitive front-desk questions without trying to replace concierge expertise.

## Pilot proposal

A low-friction 30-day pilot with a hotel-specific QR code.

Suggested placements:
- Concierge / reception counter
- Discreet in-room card
- Optional key-card sleeve or welcome material

Velvet Passport supplies the digital artwork and hotel-specific QR assets. The hotel prints locally if it chooses to use physical collateral.

The QR opens the Paris NOW experience directly. No app-store installation is required for the web experience.

## Guest-facing message

**What should you do next in Paris?**

Scan NOW for intelligent suggestions based on where you are, the time you have and what is happening around you.

## Concierge-facing message

**Extend your guest service beyond the front desk.**

NOW helps guests between concierge interactions: while walking through Paris, when weather changes, when transit becomes inconvenient, when they have unexpected free time, or when they need a practical stop such as food, water, a pharmacy or a restroom.

NOW should never be presented as replacing the concierge. The partnership language should make the relationship explicit: concierge expertise remains the premium human layer; NOW provides continuity outside the hotel.

## Operational burden

Target burden for the hotel: near zero.

- No staff software to operate for the basic pilot.
- No inventory upload required from the hotel.
- No recommendation quota.
- No obligation for the hotel to endorse every NOW recommendation.
- Hotel-specific QR can be activated or removed without changing the hotel’s own systems.

## Measurement

For the pilot, measure only what NOW can actually support:
- QR scans
- NOW openings / activations attributable to the hotel QR
- usage of the experience after activation, where available

Do not promise purchase-conversion attribution until that tracking is technically verified.

The property has independently passed the current NOW Lodging Trust Gate. A future commercial relationship must not bypass or freeze that status. If recent evidence later triggers a review or rejection, the recommendation status can change independently of the partnership.
